RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) retrieves external knowledge before generating a final answer.
A minimal RAG flow usually includes: chunking documents, embedding chunks, retrieving top-k chunks, and composing an answer from context.
Chunking improves retrieval granularity and helps avoid semantic dilution from very long documents.
Hash-based embedding is lightweight, deterministic, and works without external model APIs.
Cosine similarity is commonly used to rank retrieved chunks by vector similarity.
Agents can orchestrate retrieval tools and pass selected context to an LLM for grounded responses.
